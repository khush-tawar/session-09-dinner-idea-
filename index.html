Spaghetti Carbonara

The Classic Italian Comfort Food

Creamy, rich, and absolutely delicious - this authentic Roman pasta dish is my all-time favorite dinner

About This Dish

Spaghetti Carbonara is a traditional Italian pasta dish from Rome, known for its rich, creamy sauce made without cream! The secret lies in the combination of eggs, Pecorino Romano cheese, guanciale (cured pork jowl), and black pepper.

This dish has been my favorite since I first tasted it in a small trattoria in Rome. The simplicity of the ingredients combined with the perfect technique creates a magical dining experience that never fails to comfort and satisfy.

What makes this recipe special is its authenticity - no cream, no garlic, just pure, traditional ingredients that let each flavor shine through.

Ingredients

For the Pasta

  • 400g Spaghetti (or other long pasta)
  • - Salt for pasta water

For the Sauce

  • 4 large Egg yolks (room temperature)
  • 1 whole Egg
  • 100g Pecorino Romano cheese (finely grated)
  • 50g Parmigiano Reggiano (finely grated)
  • 200g Guanciale (or pancetta), diced
  • - Freshly ground black pepper (generous amount)

Recipe Steps

1

Prepare the Ingredients

Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. While waiting, separate egg yolks from whites and combine yolks with one whole egg in a bowl. Add grated Pecorino and Parmigiano, plus a generous amount of freshly ground black pepper. Mix well to create a thick paste.

2

Cook the Guanciale

Cut guanciale into small cubes or strips. In a large skillet over medium heat, add the guanciale (no oil needed). Cook until the fat renders and the guanciale becomes crispy, about 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.

3

Cook the Pasta

Add spaghetti to the boiling water and cook until al dente (usually 1-2 minutes less than package instructions). Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining.

4

Combine Everything

Add the drained pasta to the skillet with guanciale (off heat). Toss quickly to coat. Add the egg mixture and toss vigorously, adding splashes of pasta water to create a creamy, silky sauce. The residual heat will cook the eggs without scrambling them.

5

Serve Immediately

Plate the carbonara while hot, finishing with extra grated Pecorino and a generous grind of black pepper. Serve immediately for the best texture and flavor.

Pro Tips

🥚

Temperature Matters

Use room temperature eggs and work off the heat when combining with pasta to avoid scrambling. The residual heat from the pasta is enough to cook the eggs perfectly.

🧀

Quality Cheese

Invest in authentic Pecorino Romano and Parmigiano Reggiano. Grate them finely yourself - pre-grated cheese contains anti-caking agents that prevent a smooth sauce.

🥓

Authentic Guanciale

If you can find guanciale (cured pork jowl), use it! It's traditional and has a unique flavor. If not available, pancetta is a good substitute. Bacon works but changes the flavor profile.

💧

Save That Water

Pasta water is liquid gold! The starch helps emulsify the sauce. Always reserve at least a cup before draining, and add it gradually to achieve the perfect creamy consistency.

⚡

Work Quickly

Timing is crucial. Have everything ready before you start, and work fast once the pasta is cooked. Carbonara waits for no one - serve immediately while it's hot and creamy.

🌶️

Pepper Power

Don't skimp on the black pepper! It's one of the four essential ingredients. Use freshly ground pepper for the best flavor and add a generous amount both in the sauce and as garnish.
